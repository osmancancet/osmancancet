### About

I am a lecturer in the **Big Data Analytics Program** (Department of Statistics) at Manisa Technical Sciences Vocational School, **Manisa Celal Bayar University**, and since 2026 the **Deputy Director of the university's Computer Research and Application Center (BUAM)**.

My research sits where security meets language and data: phishing and social-engineering detection, Turkish NLP corpora, IoT and smart-grid security, and explainable AI for financial decision-making. Before academia I worked as a software engineer on machine-learning and data-analysis products, and I still build and ship the tools I teach with.

### Research interests

- **Cybersecurity:** phishing detection, social engineering, IoT and smart-grid security, intrusion detection
- **Natural Language Processing:** transformer models, Turkish-language corpora, data-leakage auditing
- **Explainable AI:** interpretable models for trading signals and security decisions
- **Financial data science:** cryptocurrency and Borsa Istanbul analytics, backtesting
